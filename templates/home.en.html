Zigbee, Thread or Wi-Fi?

The most common question by email. The short answer: use all three, each where it belongs.

CriterionZigbeeThread / MatterWi-Fi
Power useTiny — batteries last yearsTinyHigh — needs mains power
Mesh networkYes, through mains-powered devicesYes, nativeNo (depends on the router)
Needs a bridgeUSB coordinatorBorder router (Apple TV, HomePod, ZBT…)No
MaturityVery highEvolving fastHigh
Best forSensors, buttons, valvesNew cross-ecosystem devicesCameras, TVs, vacuums

Rule of thumb: if it runs on a battery, prefer Zigbee or Thread. If it needs bandwidth (video, audio), Wi-Fi.

Local automation is not ideology, it is engineering

A house that needs the cloud to switch on a light fails when the internet drops, when the vendor shuts the service down and when the app changes hands. The model defended here is simple: control lives inside the house, the cloud is optional, and every device needs a plan B. It is cheaper, faster and far more predictable.
