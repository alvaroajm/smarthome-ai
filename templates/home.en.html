06 / Understand the connections

Every connection has its place.

Zigbee, Thread and Wi-Fi are networks. Matter is an interoperability layer that can use Thread or Wi-Fi/Ethernet.

◈

Zigbee

A mesh network common in sensors, bulbs and buttons. Uses a compatible coordinator.

Explore Zigbee →
◎

Thread + Matter

Thread provides the low-power network. Matter-over-Thread devices need a border router and a Matter controller.

Understand the difference →
⌁

Wi-Fi / Ethernet

IP connectivity for TVs, cameras, panels and other devices. Local control depends on the device and integration.

Plan your network →
