## What to include in a technical question

The more context, the more useful the answer. If you can, include:

1. **Where Home Assistant runs** (Green, Raspberry Pi, mini PC, virtual machine) and the version
   (*Settings → About*).
2. **Which integration** is involved (ZHA, Zigbee2MQTT, Matter, ESPHome, HomeKit, MQTT...) and the
   **coordinator or dongle** in use (ZBT-1, ZBT-2, Sonoff, other).
3. **The exact device model** and, if available, the manufacturer's link.
4. **What you expected and what happened**, with the error message or a log excerpt
   (*Settings → System → Logs*). Remove passwords, tokens and external addresses before sending.
5. **What you have already tried.**

!!! atencao
    Do not send passwords, access tokens, API keys, Matter pairing QR codes or screenshots showing
    your external addresses. None of that is needed to answer a question.
