**SmartHome-AI** started from a common frustration: most smart home content is either product marketing, or
so technical that it assumes you already know what an MQTT broker is.

The commitment here is different: **explain the why before the how**, with examples that work in real homes —
including homes with unstable internet, thick walls and people who would rather not think about technology.

## Editorial principles

1. **Local first.** The cloud is a convenience, never a dependency.
2. **Open source whenever possible.** Home Assistant, ESPHome, Zigbee2MQTT, Frigate, Scrypted.
3. **No sponsored lists.** When a product is named, it is because it solves a described problem.
4. **Plan B matters.** Every automation must work — or fail safely — when something goes down.
5. **Respect for the people living there.** Automation that annoys the household is bad automation.

## Topics covered

- [Home Assistant](/home-assistant/) — the platform
- [ESPHome and ESP32](/esphome-esp32/) — devices you build yourself
- [Zigbee2MQTT and ZHA](/zigbee/) and the [ZBT-1 / ZBT-2 dongles](/zbt-dongles/)
- [Matter and Thread](/matter-thread/) — the standard that crosses ecosystems
- [MQTT](/mqtt/) — the postal service of the smart home
- [Apple HomeKit](/apple-homekit/) — iPhone, Siri and Apple TV
- [Scrypted, Frigate and cameras](/scrypted/) and [AI cameras](/cameras-ia/)
- [Claude and MCP](/claude-mcp/) and [OpenClaw](/openclaw/) — AI at home
- [Hardware](/hardware/), [networking](/rede-wifi/) and [CasaOS / umbrelOS](/casaos-umbrel/)

## About the language

The guide library is written in Brazilian Portuguese and is being translated into English progressively.
Configuration files, YAML and command examples are language-neutral, and browser translation handles these
pages well in the meantime.

## How this site is built

This is a static site: HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no frameworks, generated by a **Python** script from
Markdown files, versioned on **GitHub** and published on **Cloudflare**. No database, no trackers, no ads —
it loads fast even on a bad mobile connection.
