This policy applies to **smarthome-ai.com**. The site is operated by **Dr. Álvaro Menezes**.
For privacy questions, contact [admin@alvaro-menezes.com](mailto:admin@alvaro-menezes.com).

## Browsing and local preferences

The site provides public guides without reader registration. Your theme preference and the lighting
demonstration state are stored locally in your browser. The demonstration does not control real
devices or send your home configuration to the site. You can clear these preferences through your
browser's site-data settings.

## Hosting and security

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. To deliver pages and protect the service, Cloudflare may
process technical connection data, such as IP address, browser, requested page and time.
See [Cloudflare's Privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for this provider's practices.

## Traffic metrics

The site uses **Google Analytics 4** to measure visits and understand which guides are used.
The service may use cookies and browser identifiers and receive information about page views,
interactions, device and traffic source. These metrics help improve content and navigation.
See [how Google uses data from partner sites and apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Advertising

SmartHome-AI includes **Google AdSense** integration. Ad display depends on Google's approval and
the site's account settings; the presence of ad code does not mean the site has been approved.

When advertising features load, third parties, including Google and its partners, may set and read
cookies, use pixels or web beacons, and process IP addresses and other identifiers to deliver ads,
measure performance and prevent fraud. Advertising cookies may enable personalized ads based on
previous visits to this site or other sites.

You can adjust or disable personalization in [Google's My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/).
For choices offered by other participating providers, see [YourAdChoices](https://optout.aboutads.info/).
You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings. These choices do not necessarily
stop all advertisements from appearing.

## Links and contact

Links to manufacturers, projects, social networks, WhatsApp and other sites lead to services with
their own policies. If you contact us, the content and details you send will be processed by the
chosen service and the site operator to respond to your request. Avoid sending passwords,
access codes or unnecessary personal information.

## Requests and updates

To ask questions or exercise rights concerning data under the site's responsibility, use the contact
above. Data held by third parties may also require a request directly to that provider.
This policy will be updated when the services and uses described here change.
